# Lived spaces verification

Open [the slideshow](index.html) directly from disk. Its eight frames are also available as individual PNGs. The [manifest](manifest.json) identifies the source commit and records the action, expected observation and dimensions of each frame.

On November 25, 2026 the example compiled with Apple Clang and warnings treated as errors. The replay passed on the synthetic exhibition fixture in a 920 × 585 pt macOS AppKit window at 2×. Each frame is a capture of that window’s content view during the replay. The scene and score are drawn from the same session. The checks read the score, history, sightline, envelope, native responder and selection after driving the controls.

All eight frames were inspected. The two routes diverged after moving the partition, the cue peak moved to the playhead, and the undone state recovered the initial geometry and envelope. The rejected edit, restored checkpoint, width definition and selected term were visible in their respective frames. In local headless Chrome, all embedded images decoded and previous/next wraparound, right-arrow navigation and jumping from the contact sheet passed.

[Launch and replay instructions](../../../examples/lived-spaces/README.md) include the storyboard and open gates. The local check covered this consumer. The repository-wide suite, independent human assessment and physical or external validation were skipped.
