# Orchard Commons • static alternatives

One plot serves one use. The grove and play allocations are fixed per branch. Gardens take the remaining supply. Fern requests gardens, Ash requests grove and Pip requests play. Each shortfall is the number of plots below that person's minimum.

## Opening comparison

Supply is 12. Fern requests at least 6 garden plots, Ash 5 grove plots and Pip 4 play plots.

| Branch | Gardens | Grove | Play | Fern | Ash | Pip |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Orchard | 6 | 3 | 3 | Met | Short 2 | Short 1 |
| Glade | 3 | 6 | 3 | Short 3 | Met | Short 1 |
| Commons | 4 | 4 | 4 | Short 2 | Short 1 | Met |
