# V09 — Compose how a space is experienced

Let designers author the changing experience of a place: movement, visibility,
light, sound, and encounters over time. Geometry becomes one part of a spatial
score that can be walked, inspected, and revised.

## Experience

An exhibition designer sketches a visitor path, marks where an object should
first become visible, and shapes the rise and fall of light along the journey.
Move a partition and see which sightlines break. Pull the desired reveal point
along a timeline, preview candidate changes to a light cue, and compare routes
for visitors moving at different speeds. Keyboard navigation reaches rooms,
path segments, and cues in meaningful order.

The horizon includes installations, stage spaces, museums, game environments,
and responsive interiors. Authors could compose events and relationships in
space while retaining the spatial model that produces them.

## What the combination enables

3D makes spatial relationships inspectable; graphs describe adjacency and cues;
plots describe experience over time or distance; structured expressions encode
bounded rules. A path can be authored intent or a recorded route, with its role
made explicit. A lighting preview can be a stylized design aid without claiming
photometric accuracy. Audio, crowd behavior, and physical environmental analysis
would each need their own validated models.

## First question to test

Can a designer reliably create a timed reveal by editing both spatial and
temporal representations? Use one small scene, two routes, a line-of-sight test,
and one light envelope. Compare a spatial edit with a timing edit and test undo
across both. Evaluate whether the integrated view helps the designer explain
the tradeoff. Start with a local desktop preview; sensors, building control,
and immersive hardware are unnecessary for this experiment.

## Assessment and uncertainty

Agency **4/5** · Expression **4/5** · Transfer **3/5**.
Probe cost **High**: meaningful 3D hosting, picking, spatial tests, and timeline
coordination must meet in one interaction. Full environmental simulation is
substantially beyond the probe.

Potential confidence: **Low**. A designer improving a spatial sequence through
both representations would strengthen it; a workflow that always collapses
back into isolated geometry editing would weaken it.
