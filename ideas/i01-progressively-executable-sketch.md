# I01 — A sketch that gradually becomes an instrument

Let a loose sketch become operational one small region at a time.

Status: speculative product concept, captured October 20, 2026.
Origin: First brainstorm, idea 1. This is an idea brief, not an implementation commitment.

Portfolio status: retained from the earlier brainstorm. Its assessment below
uses the superseded implementation-oriented rubric. See the
[current portfolio](README.md#product-visions) for broader product visions and
[framework questions](FRAMEWORK.md) for supporting mechanisms.

## Experience

Sketch a kinetic sculpture as branches for arms, joints, weights, materials, and motion. Give one arm a length, attach an equation, and connect two joints. That region starts moving and exposing measurements while neighboring branches remain questions, pictures, or incomplete expressions. The sketch gradually becomes the sculpture's control panel.

Relevant foundations: [Proposed mind mapping](../docs/design/mind-mapping.md), [Proposed math editing](../docs/design/math-editing.md), [Proposed canvas interactions](../docs/design/canvas-interaction.md), [Proposed 3D interfaces](../docs/design/3d-interaction.md).

Define explicit transitions from informal content to executable domain objects. An informal link must never silently become a dependency. Preserve incomplete notation and partially formalized documents; no universal solver is assumed.

## Smallest useful experiment

Formalize one arm and joint beside an unfinished note. Change its length, observe motion, then undo the formalization without losing the note or its position.

Confidence: **Moderate**. Raise confidence if users can formalize a local region without constructing a full application schema; lower it if every edit demands global consistency.
