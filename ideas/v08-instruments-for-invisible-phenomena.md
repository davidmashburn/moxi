# V08 — Make invisible phenomena manipulable

Build instruments that give probability, dynamical systems, optimization, and
high-dimensional relationships useful perceptual forms. People could develop
intuition by acting on mathematical objects rather than only reading results.

## Experience

Shape a probability distribution as a mass that can be moved, split, or spread.
Pin its mean, change its tail, and watch the permitted changes respond. Select
a conditional slice and compare it with the whole. In another instrument, move
an initial condition through a phase portrait and follow the resulting family
of trajectories. Keyboard controls expose exact constraints and selected
regions, so continuous gestures have precise, repeatable equivalents.

The horizon is a family of mathematical instruments with distinct perceptual
vocabularies: uncertainty as allocation, optimization as traversable terrain,
and transformations as motion. Each metaphor earns its meaning separately.

## What the combination enables

Plots become editable mathematical surfaces, math exposes exact definitions,
3D can make selected relationships spatial, and a canvas composes instruments.
The interaction layer communicates constraints through snapping, bounded motion,
and ghost alternatives. These are designed mappings, not literal physical
properties of abstract mathematics. Projection, approximation, and hidden
dimensions remain inspectable; a vivid surface must not imply information it
cannot represent.

## First question to test

Can a person intentionally reshape uncertainty while understanding what remains
fixed? Start with a finite probability mass function, normalization, an optional
fixed mean, and a textual definition. Compare direct numeric editing with a
redistribution gesture that previews its effect before committing. Include an
infeasible edit and ask the user to explain the rejection. This tests one
metaphor without requiring a general symbolic solver or 3D renderer.

## Assessment and uncertainty

Agency **5/5** · Expression **5/5** · Transfer **4/5**.
Probe cost **Medium**: constraint-preserving gestures and faithful mathematical
feedback need new work. Other phenomena require their own instruments and tests.

Potential confidence: **Moderate**. Accurate predictions after manipulation
would strengthen it. Persistent misconceptions caused by the metaphor would
require changing the instrument even if it feels excellent to use.
